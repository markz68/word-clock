Settings

Letter Grid lights up five-minute phrases. Each lit dot adds one minute to the phrase, even when it says “to.” For example, “quarter to two” plus two dots means 1:47.

Uses your device’s local time. Preferences stay on this device. Works offline after the first successful load.

To install: use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home screen option. On iPhone or iPad, use Safari’s Share menu.
